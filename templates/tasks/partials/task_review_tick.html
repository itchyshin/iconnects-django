{% comment %} The Reviewed tick box of one completion-table row — ticked once a reviewer has signed the part off. Ticking reviews it; unticking withdraws the review. Expects ``s`` (one entry of views._task_status_context's assignee_states) and ``task``; it swaps #task-status-block. The same rules as before: only a part marked done can be reviewed, by one of the task's reviewers or a Lead (``s.can_review``); a review can be withdrawn by whoever gave it or a Lead (``s.can_unreview``). Everyone else sees it read-only. Open whether or not the task is marked completed, with one exception: a review given before the task was completed is locked (``s.review_locked``). Its box stays ticked and clickable but never toggles; a click shows the card's locked notice instead — see task_done_tick.html. A review given after completion can still be withdrawn. Likewise, a part marked done before the task was completed and left unreviewed takes no review any more (``s.review_closed``): its box is locked, unticked.
